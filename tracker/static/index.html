Engagement authorisations

The signed client authorisation behind a scan. Research mode against an external target, or with destructive/DoS testing, only runs when the project's authorisation reference names an active, unexpired record here that permits it and whose scope covers every in-scope target. Lab / internal-only research needs just a free-text self-authorisation on the project. Records are never deleted — revoke them instead; every change is audited.
